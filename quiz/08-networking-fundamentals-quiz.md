What is the default gateway used for?

<details>
<summary><b>It routes traffic to networks outside your local network</b></summary>
Correct
When a destination is not local, traffic goes to the gateway (often your router).
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>It assigns IP addresses to devices</b></summary>
Incorrect
That is handled by DHCP servers, not the gateway role itself.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>It encrypts all network traffic</b></summary>
Incorrect
Gateways route traffic but do not automatically encrypt it.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>It stores DNS records for the internet</b></summary>
Incorrect
DNS records are stored on DNS servers, not the gateway.
</details>
